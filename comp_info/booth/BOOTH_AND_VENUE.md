# Booth And Venue Operations

## Hard Limits

- Occupied booth volume: 2 m wide x 2 m deep x 2 m high.
- Three vertical display surfaces are provided as close to that size as
  possible.
- Optional table: approximately 120 cm x 60 cm and must remain inside.
- Maximum three chairs.
- Robot and all decorations must fit inside the booth.
- Fire and mist are prohibited.

## Recommended ATLAS Booth Zones

### Back Wall: Problem And Impact

- one-sentence museum accessibility problem;
- user research and measured evidence;
- ATLAS value proposition and slogan;
- clear before/after visitor journey.

### Side Wall 1: Engineering

- physical architecture and labelled hardware;
- local vision/audio versus cloud response diagram;
- model results, latency, FPS, and thermal results;
- privacy and failure-mode design.

### Side Wall 2: Development And Future

- prototype timeline and rejected designs;
- team roles and team-authorship evidence;
- cost, partners, deployment plan, and next steps;
- website/demo QR code with a printed text fallback.

### Table

- ATLAS mounted safely at visitor height;
- admin screen angled toward the team, not dominating the visitor experience;
- hidden, ventilated Jetson and power distribution;
- secured cables with no loops extending into walkways;
- recovery kit accessible but visually quiet.

## Venue Questions Requiring Written Answers

- [ ] Exact booth wall width, height, material, and attachment rules
- [ ] Number, type, voltage, and current limit of power outlets
- [ ] Whether power strips and extension cords require certification
- [ ] Wi-Fi availability, credentials, and device limits
- [ ] Whether personal hotspots or travel routers are permitted
- [ ] Setup and teardown windows
- [ ] Loading/unloading and package-receiving process
- [ ] Whether the optional table/chairs require advance reservation
- [ ] Overnight equipment storage and security
- [ ] Whether equipment may stay powered overnight
- [ ] Repair/soldering area and tool restrictions
- [ ] Final report/video upload deadline and portal
- [ ] Required badges and access for supporters
- [ ] Emergency and first-aid procedure

## Full-Scale Booth Test

1. Tape a 2 m x 2 m square on the floor and mark 2 m height.
2. Place the exact table, chairs, team members, robot, bags, and posters.
3. Verify that visitors and judges can see the demo without blocking the aisle.
4. Run the presentation while another person photographs every angle.
5. Check glare, noise, ventilation, cable hazards, and access to recovery parts.
6. Time assembly and disassembly.
7. Pack everything in the actual travel cases and verify bag limits.

## Daily Venue Procedure

- Photograph the booth and cable connections after setup.
- Run preflight before judges or visitors arrive.
- Maintain required booth presence.
- Keep food/drinks away from electronics.
- Log every fault and replacement part used.
- Shut down, inventory, and secure equipment using organizer instructions.
