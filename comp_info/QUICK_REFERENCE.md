## Five-Minute Pre-Demo Check

1. Check power, cooling, and cable strain relief.
2. Start ATLAS with the documented competition command.
3. Confirm admin dashboard health indicators.
4. Confirm camera frame, artwork detection, microphone, and output device.
5. Run one question, one follow-up, one artwork identification, and one
   language switch.
6. Place ATLAS in the correct ready state before judges arrive.
7. Keep the recovery USB and printed recovery card within reach.

## Failure Decision

- Audio failure: change output device, then use speaker fallback.
- Cloud voice failure: use one consistent Piper voice.
- Camera failure: keep the dashboard and voice demo running; reconnect camera
  only after the current explanation.
- Internet failure: use local vision, local STT/TTS, and the prepared offline
  explanation; do not claim full cloud functionality.
- Full runtime failure: perform one clean restart. If it does not recover within
  the rehearsed limit, show the recorded proof while one team member follows
  the recovery checklist.
