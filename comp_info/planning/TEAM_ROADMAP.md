packing and final checks ahead of the event.

## September 2-19: Working Prototype And Ownership

- Finish mechanical integration and camera cooling.
- Receive the trained model but independently evaluate and reproduce its setup.
- Begin the AI-use register and competition evidence archive.
- Establish baseline latency, FPS, recognition, temperature, and runtime.
- Confirm age group, qualification, registration, and organizer contacts.

Exit check: ATLAS completes the full demo sequence on the target hardware and
the team can explain every major component.

## September 20-October 18: Real-World Validation

- Test with students, families, visitors, accessibility users, and a museum
  professional.
- Convert observations into measured results and design changes.
- Complete the first business model and competitor comparison.
- Build booth mock-up version 1.
- Draft report sections continuously instead of waiting for November.

Exit check: evidence supports the problem, need, impact, and technical choices.

## October 19-November 7: Feature Freeze

- Stop adding optional features.
- Run full reliability, thermal, camera, audio, and network tests.
- Finish booth design and packable dimensions.
- Book/confirm travel and complete documents for minors and equipment.
- Produce the first complete 90-second video and 5-minute presentation.

Exit check: the same demo succeeds repeatedly after a cold boot.

## November 8-15: Competition Release Candidate

- Create release candidate, Jetson image, dependency lock, and restore media.
- Complete report and video drafts.
- Run mock judging with teachers and people unfamiliar with ATLAS.
- Correct weak answers, unclear evidence, and unreliable interactions.

Exit check: release candidate, report, video, and booth can be judged as-is.

## November 16-22: Submission Ready

- Finalize report/video before the organizer's real deadline.
- Run three adversarial judge sessions.
- Complete customs, battery, baggage, insurance, and transport paperwork.
- Perform timed booth assembly and technical recovery.

Exit check: no required deliverable depends on unfinished development.

## November 23-29: Hard Freeze

- Accept only fixes for reproduced, competition-blocking defects.
- Tag the final release and preserve all hashes/backups.
- Print reports and booth materials.
- Finalize bag ownership and weigh every bag.

Exit check: ATLAS is packed in a fully tested configuration.

## November 30-December 4: Final Verification

- Check online Q&A and final organizer instructions.
- Confirm flights, hotel, transfers, venue access, and emergency contacts.
- Charge/test batteries and every spare.
- Verify all backups from a separate computer.
- Complete one final dress rehearsal without code changes afterward.

## December 5-6: Travel And Puerto Rico Rebuild

- Arrive with a buffer before the published December 8 start.
- Inspect baggage immediately and document any damage.
- Reassemble and test ATLAS at the hotel using the competition configuration.
- Acclimate electronics before powering them after temperature/humidity changes.

## December 8-10: Event

- December 8: setup, practice, coach meeting, and opening activities according
  to the current draft schedule.
- December 9-10: judging, public demonstrations, and ceremonies.
- Keep at least one team member at the booth when required.
- Record faults and fixes, but do not experiment with untested changes.
