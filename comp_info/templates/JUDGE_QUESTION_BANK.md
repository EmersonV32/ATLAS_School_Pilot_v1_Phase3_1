# ATLAS Judge Question Bank

Answer these aloud without the coach. Add evidence beside each answer in the
team's private rehearsal notes.

## Problem And Users

1. What exact museum problem does ATLAS solve?
2. Who confirmed that this problem exists?
3. Why is a headset better than an app, audio guide, or human guide?
4. Which users benefit most, and who might be excluded?
5. What measurable improvement did testing show?
6. What negative effects could ATLAS create?

## Innovation And Theme

1. What is the single most innovative ATLAS capability?
2. How does it fit Robots Meet Culture and Area 3?
3. What exists already, and why is ATLAS meaningfully different?
4. Why is this a robot rather than only an AI application?

## Hardware And Engineering

1. Why did you choose the Jetson and this camera?
2. What sensors, actuators, and autonomous decisions does ATLAS use?
3. How did you solve overheating, power, mounting, and cable problems?
4. What breaks first during long operation?
5. Which component would you redesign for production?

## AI And Software

1. How was the artwork dataset created and split?
2. What do precision, recall, mAP, and false positives mean for your visitor?
3. How did you prevent data leakage between training and validation images?
4. Why did you choose this model and deployment backend?
5. Which processing is local and which is cloud-based?
6. What happens when internet, Cartesia, Gemini, or the camera fails?
7. How do you prevent voices or languages changing mid-response?
8. How does conversational context work and when is it deleted?
9. What did AI assistants contribute to development?
10. Can every team member explain the code and training decisions?

## Privacy, Safety, And Rights

1. Does ATLAS store faces, video, audio, names, or conversations?
2. What information is sent to third parties?
3. How are children and museum visitors informed or protected?
4. Where did the artwork images come from and may you use them?
5. How are batteries, heat, cables, and hearing volume kept safe?

## Business And Future

1. Who purchases ATLAS and who uses it?
2. What does one complete unit cost today?
3. What would cost change at 10, 100, and 1,000 units?
4. What recurring cloud, maintenance, and content costs exist?
5. How would a museum add its collection and languages?
6. Who are the required partners?
7. What would you accomplish in the next 3, 6, and 12 months?
8. What evidence would be required before a museum deployment?

## Failure Challenge

Ask the team to demonstrate or explain:

- wrong artwork detection;
- no network;
- disconnected camera;
- unavailable headset;
- overheating;
- multilingual visitor changing language mid-session;
- complete recovery from a fresh boot.
