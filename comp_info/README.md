# ATLAS Competition Information Hub

Last verified: 2026-09-02

This folder is the quick-access competition binder for ATLAS at the WRO 2026
International Final in San Juan, Puerto Rico. It is designed to remain useful
before departure, during setup, and while troubleshooting at the event.

## Start Here

1. On a normal preparation day, open
   [planning/MASTER_CHECKLIST.md](planning/MASTER_CHECKLIST.md).
2. During the final month, follow
   [planning/TEAM_ROADMAP.md](planning/TEAM_ROADMAP.md).
3. While packing, use
   [travel/PACKING_AND_EQUIPMENT.md](travel/PACKING_AND_EQUIPMENT.md) and fill
   [templates/EQUIPMENT_MANIFEST.csv](templates/EQUIPMENT_MANIFEST.csv).
4. At the venue, open [QUICK_REFERENCE.md](QUICK_REFERENCE.md).
5. If ATLAS fails, use
   [technical/COMPETITION_RECOVERY.md](technical/COMPETITION_RECOVERY.md).

## Confirmed Event Facts

- Event: WRO International Final 2026
- Category: Future Innovators
- Theme: Robots Meet Culture
- Location: Puerto Rico Convention Center, San Juan, Puerto Rico
- Published event dates: December 8-10, 2026
- Published event fee: USD 375 per participant, coach, or supporter, before
  possible local taxes or administration fees
- International booth: 2 m x 2 m x 2 m
- Optional table: approximately 120 cm x 60 cm
- Judging session: 5-minute presentation and demonstration, then 5 minutes of
  questions

The event schedule is still described as a draft by the organizer. The team
must confirm arrival, registration, submission, and setup details through the
Canadian National Organizer before purchasing non-refundable travel.
