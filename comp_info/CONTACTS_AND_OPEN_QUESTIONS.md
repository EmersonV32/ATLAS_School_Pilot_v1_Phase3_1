## Questions For Canadian National Organizer

- What is the team's confirmed age group and international slot status?
- What are registration, payment, report, and video deadlines?
- What portal/account submits report and video?
- What travel, hotel, and ground transport are handled by the delegation?
- What dates and times must the delegation arrive and depart?
- May an outside person run the team's fixed model-training script on borrowed
  compute without changing any team-authored decisions?
- Are there Canadian adaptations or extra documents that remain relevant at
  the international final?
- What insurance and supervision requirements apply to student travellers?

## Questions For WRO 2026 Host

- Exact setup and teardown windows?
- Booth wall dimensions/material and permitted attachment methods?
- Outlet quantity, voltage/current, and power-strip rules?
- Venue Wi-Fi and hotspot policy?
- Table/chair reservation process?
- Overnight storage/security and powered-device policy?
- Loading, package delivery, and airport-transfer options?
- Repair area and tool/soldering rules?
- Accreditation and access for supporters?
