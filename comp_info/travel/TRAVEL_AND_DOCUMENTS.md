# Travel, Entry, Customs, And Transport

Last verified: 2026-09-02. Always recheck government and airline rules.

## Entry To Puerto Rico

Puerto Rico is a United States territory and follows US entry requirements.

- A Canadian citizen travelling with a Canadian passport does not need ESTA.
- A traveller who is not a Canadian or US citizen may need ESTA or a visa.
- A Canadian passport should be valid for the duration of the stay, but an
  airline may apply stricter boarding rules.
- Customs may request a return/onward ticket and proof of sufficient funds.

Check each traveller independently:

- [Government of Canada Puerto Rico advice](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/puerto-rico)
- [CBP guidance for Canadian citizens](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1441?language=en_US)
- [Official ESTA site](https://esta.cbp.dhs.gov/)

## Minors

For every traveller under 19 who is not accompanied by both parents or all
legal guardians, carry:

- individual passport;
- original signed travel consent letter;
- itinerary and return date;
- names and contact information of parents/guardians;
- accompanying adult's information;
- copy of birth/citizenship certificate;
- relevant custody orders or other supporting documents;
- medical authorization and important health information.

Government of Canada strongly recommends notarizing the consent letter and
carrying the original.

- [Consent letter guidance and template](https://travel.gc.ca/travelling/children/consent-letter)
- [Travel documents for children](https://travel.gc.ca/travelling/children/children-travel)

## Insurance And Emergency Preparation

- Travel medical insurance covering the full trip and medical evacuation.
- Equipment insurance that covers checked baggage and temporary exhibition use.
- Copies of passports, insurance, itinerary, and organizer contacts stored
  separately and with a parent/guardian in Canada.
- Emergency funds that are not all held by one person.
- Register the trip through
  [Registration of Canadians Abroad](https://travel.gc.ca/travelling/registration).

## Temporary Export From Canada

Create a manifest with description, serial number, country of origin if known,
owner, value, and photograph for every important item.

### BSF407

CBSA can document uniquely identifiable personal effects before departure on
Form BSF407. The service is free and helps prove that returning items were
taken out of Canada rather than purchased abroad.

- Present the equipment at a CBSA office before departure.
- Keep the wallet-size document with the travel documents.
- It has no expiry while the information remains legible.

[CBSA temporary export guidance](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/publications/dm-md/d2/d2-6-5-eng.pdf)

### ATA Carnet / Formal Temporary Import

An ATA Carnet can simplify temporary entry of professional or exhibition
equipment. It costs money and requires correct customs stamping when goods
enter and leave. Contact the Canadian Chamber of Commerce or a customs broker
if the equipment is high-value, shipped separately, or treated as commercial
exhibition equipment.

CBP specifically recommends considering a Carnet or temporary import bond for
trade-show supplies that are shipped or valued above USD 2,500. Obtain written
advice for ATLAS rather than assuming that personal baggage treatment applies.

- [CBP trade-show checklist](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article1286?language=en_US)
- [CBSA ATA Carnet information](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tip-pec-eng.html)

## Flights And Ground Transport

- Prefer changeable tickets because the published schedule remains a draft.
- Avoid a connection that leaves no same-day recovery option.
- Carry critical electronics and recovery media across multiple team members.
- Book a vehicle that can carry the whole team and all equipment cases.
- Confirm that the hotel can hold equipment and receive packages.
- Do not ship the only copy of any required component.
- Photograph baggage before check-in and immediately after any damage.

## Accommodation And Meals

The host says accommodation is not included. Confirm the Canadian delegation's
arrangements before booking. The official site currently lists:

- Aloft San Juan: 1 minute from event, breakfast not included;
- Hyatt House San Juan: 8 minutes, breakfast included;
- Hyatt Place San Juan/City Center: 8 minutes, breakfast included;
- additional listed options in Condado and the convention district.

Published registration includes lunch and dinner December 8-9 and lunch on
December 10. Budget for all breakfasts, arrival-day meals, and dinner on
December 10 unless the delegation confirms otherwise.
