# Packing And Equipment Plan

Complete the equipment manifest before assigning bags.

## Carry-On: Never Lose These

- Jetson and primary boot/storage device
- primary camera and headset
- essential power supplies and audio adapters
- final model weights and local knowledge base
- encrypted recovery SSD/USB
- laptop needed to administer or restore ATLAS
- spare lithium batteries and power banks within airline limits
- passports, consent letters, insurance, customs documents
- one printed report and digital booth files

Split these across team members so that one delayed bag does not destroy the
demo. Keep a written map showing which person has each item.

## Checked Baggage

- booth fabric/posters and non-critical decorations
- permitted tools and soldering iron packaged under airline rules
- replacement printed reports
- duplicate cables, mounts, fasteners, tape, and cable covers
- low-value spare enclosures and 3D-printed parts

Do not put power banks or loose spare lithium batteries in checked baggage.

## Battery Rules

- Record voltage and capacity for every battery.
- Calculate Wh as `V x Ah`, or `V x (mAh / 1000)`.
- Prefer batteries at or below 100 Wh.
- Batteries between 101 and 160 Wh generally require airline approval and may
  be limited in quantity.
- Protect each spare battery's terminals and prevent crushing or activation.
- Never travel with a damaged, swollen, or recalled battery.
- Check the selected airline because it may be stricter than CATSA.

[CATSA battery guidance](https://www.catsa-acsta.gc.ca/en/what-can-bring/guidelines-batteries)

## Repair Kit

- tested USB, USB-C, HDMI, Ethernet, and audio cables
- powered USB hub and required adapters
- replacement camera and tested audio fallback
- cooling fan, thermal pads, and safe fasteners
- hook-and-loop straps, cable ties, gaffer tape, labels
- small screwdrivers, hex keys, pliers, and cutters if permitted
- spare printed case/mount parts
- multimeter with battery removed or secured as required
- compact first-aid kit separate from electronics

CATSA lists soldering irons as prohibited in carry-on and conditional in
checked baggage. The heat-producing component or energy source must be removed
or isolated to prevent activation.

[CATSA soldering-iron guidance](https://www.catsa-acsta.gc.ca/en/what-can-bring/item/soldering-irons)

## Final Bag Check

- [ ] Every item is on the manifest.
- [ ] Every serial number and high-value item has a photo.
- [ ] Every battery has visible Wh information.
- [ ] Critical items are distributed across carry-ons.
- [ ] Checked tools comply with airline and security rules.
- [ ] Bags are below the selected airline's weight and size limits.
- [ ] Cases are labelled inside and outside with team contact information.
- [ ] No API keys or unencrypted secrets are printed or stored openly.
- [ ] One parent/coach has the complete bag assignment list.
